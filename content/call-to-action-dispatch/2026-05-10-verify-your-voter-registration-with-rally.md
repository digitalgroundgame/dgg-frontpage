## Don't know how to use our new Rally tool?

Follow the steps below for the website.

### Step 1

Go to [digitalgroundgame.org/register](https://digitalgroundgame.org/register) and click Go.

![The Rally registration tool start screen](/uploads/rally-register-step-1.webp)

### Step 2

Fill out the information and click "check registration."

![The Rally registration information form](/uploads/rally-register-step-2.webp)

### Step 3

Click "This is me!" with the match that has your information to confirm it's you. If it's not you, click "show more potential matches" until you find your information, then click "This is me!"

![The Rally voter match confirmation screen](/uploads/rally-register-step-3.webp)

### Step 4

Share the link with your friends and family to make sure their voter registration information is valid and has not been purged. Your personal link will also track how many people used the Rally tool and how many people clicked on your link, so check back to see how many people engaged with your link.

![The Rally share link screen](/uploads/rally-register-step-4.webp)

## Want to use the Discord bot?

Here's how to use it.

### Step 1

Join the Discord by going to [Discord.gg/digitalgroundgame](https://Discord.gg/digitalgroundgame), verify yourself, then go to the text channel called `#check-my-registration`.

![The Discord check-my-registration channel](/uploads/rally-discord-step-1.webp)

### Step 2

Select the red button called "Look Someone Up," then fill out the required information: first name, last name, city name, two-letter state, and your approximate age. Then click submit.

![The Discord Rally lookup form](/uploads/rally-discord-step-2.webp)

### Step 3

There will be a message below that asks if the box contains your friend. Click "yes, this is my friend" and their voter registration information will pop up. You can click "look someone up" to make sure that your friends' and family's voter information has not been purged by any voter roll purges, so stay vigilant and use this tool as much as possible.

![The Discord voter registration result confirmation](/uploads/rally-discord-step-3.webp)
