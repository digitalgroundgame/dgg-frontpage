## How to Email and Call your Representatives

### Find your elected officials through Democracy Direct.

[Democracy Direct](https://democracy-direct.com/)

Please write a email first. This will make a phone call you do easier as your thoughts and talking points will be organized.

## Writing your email

The email could be the same for all your representatives and should include the following:

1. Introduction, who you are and where you are from to show you are their constituent.
2. Your worry about how the current cap limits your representation in the U.S. House.
3. Your desire for this problem to be solved before the 2030 census.
4. Your desire to have the size of the House expanded.
5. Your interest on being in more communication with your representatives.

## Example email

*This is an example to Suzan DelBene from Washington.*

> Representative DelBene,
>
> I am a resident of Seattle and I wanted to reach out about the current cap on the U.S House of Representatives. The current 435 Rep limit does not allow you as a Representative to interact with your constituents as the framing of our system intended. The 2030 Census is 4 years away and now is the opportunity to prepare a correction. Legislation removing the US House limit is needed.
>
> The current environment distances representatives from their constituents and is one of the main causes of the current political climate.
>
> I am anxious about the current polarization and divisiveness in congress and have signed [the petition](https://nocapfund.org/) on No Cap Fund's website. I hope to hear more form you and I want to have more and deeper interactions with my representative in the future.
>
> Best wishes and thank you for your service,
>
> [Your Name]

## Calling your Representative

After writing your emails you are encouraged to call your Representative using the phone numbers they provide. Use your email as a talking points guide. Let the staffer (or potential Representative) know you emailed them and that you want to voice your concerns. If they do not pick up, leave a voicemail voicing your concern and despite talking to your Representative about it. BE RESPECTFUL!!! Use professional language and do not use profanity.
