## Keep the message direct

Pick one local issue tied to voting access, civic participation, public safety, or accountable government. Write a short message to five local officials explaining what you support, why it matters, and what action you want them to take.

[Coordinate messaging in Discord](https://discord.gg/digitalgroundgame)
{% /two-column %}

{% two-column image="/uploads/pxl_20260524_175414288.webp" alt="Phone banking workspace" layout="image-right" %}
## Share the useful responses

If an office replies with a commitment, timeline, or helpful resource, share it with your regional squad. Local responses can become talking points, campaign questions, and future organizing leads.

[Send updates to your region](https://discord.gg/digitalgroundgame)
