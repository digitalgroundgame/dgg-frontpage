# We are going to make stickers to put on Gas Pumps!!!

Learn how to quickly make and order stickers!

## Pick what sticker you want to use, or pick all of them!!

Just right click on the image and select "save image" to get what you want!

## Slap those stickers on a Gas Pump!!!

Find a gas pump and find a location to put the sticker on that is visible, highlights the high prices, and is on bare surfaces.

### IMPORTANT!!!

Do not place stickers over screens, buttons, or other stickers!!!! Only place stickers on **BARE SURFACES**!!!! We are looking to have good vibes, not break laws.

**Make sure to check local guidelines on where you can legally place stickers.**

Also feel free to use these in other ways you think it makes fun of MAGA.
