## Pick a real target

Work with your regional lead to choose a precinct, campus, neighborhood, or event where direct voter contact can make a difference. A good canvass has a clear goal, a defined turf, a script, and a follow-up plan.

[Start planning with your region](https://discord.gg/digitalgroundgame)
{% /two-column %}

{% two-column image="/group-wide.webp" alt="Digital Ground Game volunteers gathered together" layout="image-right" %}
## Build the team before the day

Confirm roles early: one person handles logistics, one tracks sign-ups, one answers new volunteer questions, and one reports results after the canvass. Good structure turns a single event into repeatable capacity.

[Join the canvassing effort](https://discord.gg/digitalgroundgame)
