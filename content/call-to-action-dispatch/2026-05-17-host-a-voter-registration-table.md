## Make registration easy

Set up a voter registration table at a campus event, community gathering, farmers market, or neighborhood meeting. Bring printed QR codes for your state registration portal, a simple sign-up sheet for follow-up, and a clear pitch: registering now keeps people ready before deadlines get stressful.

[Join the Discord to coordinate a table](https://discord.gg/digitalgroundgame)
{% /two-column %}

{% two-column image="/initiative-photo.webp" alt="People marching with signs and American flags" layout="image-right" %}
## Report back what you learn

After the event, send your region lead the number of conversations, new registrations, and common questions you heard. Those notes help us improve scripts, target future events, and build a stronger local volunteer bench.

[Find your next action in Discord](https://discord.gg/digitalgroundgame)
