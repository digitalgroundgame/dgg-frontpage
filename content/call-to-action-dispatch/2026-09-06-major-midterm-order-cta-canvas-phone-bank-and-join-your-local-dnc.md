## Phone bank


Phone banking is the universal, cheap way to canvass. Where canvassing reaches people in cities and towns, phone banking operations reach people who live in more isolated places, like forests, back roads, and reservations. You can find phone banking operations through your local Democratic Party, but if you don't want to call or show up there to get that information, you can find them through [Mobilize.us!](https://www.mobilize.us/)


If you have any questions about phone banking, please consult these two articles:

## Join your local Democratic Party


Your local Democratic Party is a great way for you to learn about the state and local offices and ordinances on the ballot in local elections! They also are the ones you'd work with on the ground when canvassing and phone banking for local candidates! They usually require some sort of fee; however, the fee tends to be on the small side (e.g., $10 for a one-year membership in my local party). Make sure you sign up because they will also let you know when candidates are going to be in town so you can network and get to know your local representatives and congressional leaders!
