## Why the SAVE Act is Dangerous

The SAVE Act cannot be looked at in a vacuum. The act is not only about voter disenfranchisement, but in the context of MAGA it is a tool in the administration's plans for interfering the results of elections, the 2026 midterms particularly. One of MAGA's core tenants is the opposition steals elections and actively engages in "voter fraud".

Central to this is the belief that the majority of the voter fraud is done by undocumented non-citizens and fraudulent ballots. This core tenant is more foundational than their immigration policy as it is one of the major motivations for deporting "criminals".

This bill allows state election officials to be prosecuted for "who registers an applicant to vote in an election for Federal office who fails to present documentary proof of United States citizenship". It is vaguely worded to allow nefarious interpretation and legal ambiguity.

This bill is something Trump wishes he had in 2020. It would have allowed him to more directly prosecute state election officials. It would have created enforcement mechanisms for his demands to access state voter roles, and it would have given him a legal pretext to "protect" the polls.

**The SAVE Act is not just a bill for voter disenfranchisement, it is a legal pretext to interfere with elections that Trump deems "stolen".**

## Writing your email

The email should be to both of your state Senators; it can be the same email sent to both or a different one for each. All emails should cover the following points:

1. Introduction, who you are and where you are from to show you are their constituent.
2. Your worry about the effects of the SAVE Act on voter disenfranchisement
3. The future vote in the Senate to pass the SAVE Act and that you urge them to vote against it.
4. Your concern about the preemptive attempts to de-legitimize elections.
5. Why do you personally care about this? For this last segment, imprint your individuality.

## Calling your Senator

After writing your emails you are encouraged to call your Senators' using the phone numbers they provide. Use your email as a talking points guide. Let the staffer (or potential Senator) know you emailed them and that you want to voice your concerns about the SAVE Act. If they do not pick up, leave a voicemail voicing your concern and despite talking to your Senator about it. **BE RESPECTFUL!!!** Use professional language and do not use profanity.
