## **The Trump admin is planning to build a [250 foot arch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Triumphal_Arch) overlooking the Potomac River on the pathway to the Washington Monument**

* This arch will directly interfere with Arlington National Cemetery

  * Construction on the arch is expected to take 3 years, as daily funeral services are held at Arlington Monday through Friday
  * The “United States Triumphal Arch” is set to be constructed at Memorial Circle on Memorial Drive between [Arlington Memorial Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington_Memorial_Bridge) and [Arlington National Cemetery](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington_National_Cemetery)
  * When asked by a reporter, Trump explicitly stated the arch was “[for me](https://www.facebook.com/watch/?v=3691604840974627).”

## **[VoteVets](https://votevets.org/about) is a national Democratic veteran advocacy group which has partnered with [Home of the Brave](https://newsletter.ofthebrave.org/), an advocacy group founded by Sarah Longwell of The Bulwark.**

* Together, they are urging those against the arch to contact their representatives and the lawmakers responsible for making a final decision on construction Sept. 30

  * Representatives Don Beyer (D-VA) and Dina Titus (D-NV) authored the [Arlington National Cemetery Viewshed Protection Act](https://www.live5news.com/2026/05/30/house-democrats-introduce-bill-block-trumps-triumphal-arch/) to prohibit construction at Memorial Circle
  * Every member of Congress can speak out against this construction, contact your [Representatives ](https://www.house.gov/representatives/find-your-representative)and [Senators](https://www.senate.gov/senators/senators-contact.htm) to let them know where we stand on [Trump's Arch](https://www.facebook.com/watch/?v=3691604840974627), and to support the Cemetery Viewshed Protection Act
