## Writing your email

Your email can be the same for all of your representatives. It should include:

1. An introduction with who you are and where you are from, so they know you are their constituent.
2. Your worry about how the current cap limits your representation in the U.S. House.
3. Your desire for this problem to be solved before the 2030 census.
4. Your desire to have the size of the House expanded.
5. Your interest in having more communication with your representatives.

{% two-column image="/uploads/stockcap.webp" alt="A stock graphic of a U.S. Capitol dome with a graduation cap" layout="image-left" %}
## Example email

This example is addressed to Representative Suzan DelBene from Washington.

Representative DelBene,

I am a resident of Seattle and I wanted to reach out about the current cap on the U.S. House of Representatives. The current 435 representative limit does not allow you, as a Representative, to interact with your constituents as the framing of our system intended. The 2030 Census is four years away, and now is the opportunity to prepare a correction. Legislation removing the U.S. House limit is needed.

The current environment distances representatives from their constituents and is one of the main causes of the current political climate.

I am anxious about the current polarization and divisiveness in Congress and have signed [the petition](http://nocapfund.org/) on No Cap Fund's website. I hope to hear more from you, and I want to have more and deeper interactions with my representative in the future.

Best wishes and thank you for your service,

[Your Name]
{% /two-column %}

## Calling your representative

After writing your emails, call your representative using the phone numbers they provide. Use your email as a talking points guide. Let the staffer, or the representative if you reach them directly, know you emailed them and that you want to voice your concerns about expanding the U.S. House.

If they do not pick up, leave a voicemail. Be respectful, use professional language, and do not use profanity.
