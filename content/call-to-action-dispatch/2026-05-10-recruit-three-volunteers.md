## Bring people into the work

This week, ask three people to take one concrete political action with you. Start with friends who already care about democracy, local issues, or winning elections, then give them a clear next step: join the Discord, attend a regional meeting, or sign up for a phone bank.

[Invite someone to Digital Ground Game](https://discord.gg/digitalgroundgame)
{% /two-column %}

{% two-column image="/group-wide.webp" alt="Digital Ground Game volunteers gathered together" layout="image-right" %}
## Make the first action small

New volunteers are more likely to stick around when the first ask is specific and achievable. Offer to sit with them through their first call, share the calendar link, or introduce them to a regional lead.

[Help grow your squad](https://discord.gg/digitalgroundgame)
