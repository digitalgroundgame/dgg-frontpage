## Contact your representatives and senators about stopping the siege on the US Forest Service!

## Trump admin takes a hatchet to yet another federal agency!

The admin has decided to move the USDA: Forest Service HQ from DC to Salt Lake City, Utah! This will require staff to either relocate or resign, which will cause a staffing shortage in the Forest Service ranks, including their R&D teams, which are important to understanding and combating wildfires under environments changing under the increased pressure of climate change!

## The Forest Service's R&D teams are being relocated from 57 facilities!

The relocation of dozens of research teams and the shuttering 57 research facilities doesn't just mean the loss of institutional knowledge, but the loss of current studies those facilities were conducting at the time will have to end. This would lead to research stagnating in a time when we need to understand how the changing conditions under climate change affect the environment that can lead to wildfires occurring!

![Smokey Bear USFS](/uploads/smokey-usfs.webp)

In truth, the Trump administration is doing this in the hopes that it would diminish yet another federal agency beyond the point of repair! One in which is very much needed as we begin our trek into what is predicted to be one of the worst wildfire seasons predicted so far!

Why is this bad? Well, you can't move a thirty-year watershed study, relocate a decades-long old-growth monitoring program, or box up a forest and ship it to Colorado. When facilities close, experiments die, datasets end, and partnerships with universities that took generations to build, as well as institutional knowledge of scientists running programs that will walk out the door, all of it will perish with the shuttering and relocating of scientists and Forest Service offices.

The US: FS R&D team is one of the world's leading wildland fire research organizations out there whose work includes how climate change can alter fuel moisture and fire behavior through warmer and drier conditions, qualities taken into consideration when determining how bad a fire season we will have that year. With summer fast approaching, the administration picked the worst time to relocate a federal agency that is critically required to combat wildfire season. Scientists will have to end their research, as they can't bring the ecosystems they conduct their research in with them, Forest Service administrators now don't have easy access to representatives and senators, and vice versa, to provide ready and quick information about the relationship between changing climate and increases in the intensity and frequency of wildfires.

This doesn't just spell disaster for our forests, this spells disaster for people who have to insure themselves from fire damages at even greater rates, causing an increase in insurance premiums and driving up the difficulty in housing affordability in an economy that has been straining since the early parts of this decade. The result will lead to higher rates of unaffordability and lead to more Americans dying, affecting both our financial safety and our physical safety!

So, what can we do? Well, it's time to make our voices known in one of three ways:

1. Phone your district congressmen/state senators and advocate for the Forest Service HQ and R&D teams to stay in DC so that congress people have the capability to be informed on the issues affecting our forests.

2. Send out an email to your local congressmen/state senator about stopping the relocating of the Forest Service HQ.

3. Phone bank for environmental causes using this site: [Get Involved | Environmental Voter Project](https://www.environmentalvoter.org/get-involved) to get out the vote for races that support environmental sustainability by phone banking!

## Writing your email

The email should be to both of your state Senators and your districts representative; it can be the same email sent to both or a different one for each. All emails should cover the following points:

1. Introduction, who you are and where you are from to show you are their constituent.
2. Your worry about the effects of the Forest Service being relocated out of DC and the shuttering of R&D facilities.
3. The future consequences of the moving of the USFS and how this will affect our ability to fight wildfires.
4. Your concern about the preemptive attempts to de-legitimize necessary big government institutions.
5. Why do you personally care about this? For this last segment, imprint your individuality.

## Calling your Senator/Representative

After writing your emails you are encouraged to call your Senators' using the phone numbers they provide. Use your email as a talking points guide. Let the staffer (or potential Senator) know you emailed them and that you want to voice your concerns about the Forest Service relocation. If they do not pick up, leave a voicemail voicing your concern and despite talking to your Senator about it. **BE RESPECTFUL!!!** Use professional language and do not use profanity.
