## Knocking doors gets people to the polls. Poll watchers make sure those votes count!

We are partnering with **[Defend 2026](https://defend2026.org)**, a voter protection organization that recruits, trains, and places poll watchers. Election Day is November 3rd. We told them Digital Ground Game could bring them volunteers from all over the country. Now we need you to prove us right!

## What we ask of you

1. **[Sign up using our link](https://defendhub.org/interest/dgg)*** Defend 2026 made this link just for Digital Ground Game. Use it so they know you came from us!
2. **Send the link to one person*** A friend, a roommate, a parent, someone from your College Democrats chapter. Know a lawyer, law student, retiree, or teacher? Start with them.
3. **Tell us you did it*** Post in the Discord once you have signed up so we can cheer you on!

## What happens after you sign up

![](/uploads/d26-how-it-works.webp)

* **Can't make it to a polling place?** You can still help. The same form lets you phone bank or text from home, or staff the hotline.
* **Where your information goes.** The form asks for your name, email, phone, and home address. Defend 2026 states your details are only shared with their team working in your area.
