## College Democrats organizers were left to answer for the national board's decision to feature Hasan Piker at the 2026 convention, one they had no part in, and we want their firsthand accounts.


## [**Share Your Account: Fill Out The Form Here**](https://forms.gle/znBfMhagwWnWchvv6)


On Saturday, the College Democrats of America (CDA) national board announced Hasan Piker as a speaker at CDA's 2026 convention, the organization's first in years. Piker opened by calling himself "an outsider currently pushing for an insurgency" inside the Democratic Party, used the speech to tell the room "we don't need those Democrats anymore," meaning the ones "who compromise, who conciliate, and who betray us," and delivered it in a Mao suit.


The board did not put that choice to state leaders or warn them it was coming. Members, state leaders, and chapter organizers found out only when CDA posted it to Instagram, days before the convention, leaving volunteers who had no role in the decision to answer for it on their campuses and in their communities.


That decision does not represent the work most College Democrats do every day. Across the country, student organizers register voters, support candidates, build campus organizations, and bring young people into politics. Those organizers, and their experiences inside the organization, deserve to be heard.


We are collecting firsthand accounts from current and former CDA members, chapter leaders, state federation leaders, and volunteers. Tell us:


- Which CDA chapter are you part of?
- Were your state or local leaders told in advance?
- Did you raise concerns? How did leadership respond?
- How has the board's decision and communication affected your chapter?
- What work is your chapter doing that deserves more attention?
- What should College Democrats do next?
