# Just because you live abroad doesn't mean that your vote doesn't travel with you! Register to vote before it's too late!

## Just because you're outside of the US doesn't mean your vote doesn't matter!

Millions of American citizens living overseas are eligible to vote but never do, often because they don't know how easy the process actually is.

![Democrats Abroad and Vote From Abroad partnership graphic](/uploads/vote-abroad-dgg-democrats-abroad-cover.webp)

Democrats Abroad has partnered with VoteFromAbroad.org, the single best tool for overseas and military voters to register, request absentee ballots, and make sure their voice reaches home on Election Day. Through this partnership, DGG is helping drive voter registration among Americans abroad. More than that, we're demonstrating the real, trackable impact we have on the political process.

![Vote from abroad information graphic](/uploads/vote-abroad-democrats-abroad-info.webp)

## Show your support by signing yourselves, or others you know who live or work abroad!

This is where you come in. We have a dedicated DGG link that lets us measure exactly how many voter registrations our community generates. Every single person who registers through our link is a direct result of this community's work, and those numbers matter. Whether you're abroad yourself, know someone who is, or just want to share the link on social media, you are the distribution engine for this initiative.
