SJELL has agreed to become a Utah State Contributor for this newsletter! For this week's newsletter these are some things he thinks you should know:

- Utah primaries are coming up on June 23 for contested seats not settled in the conventions.
  - Final registration for the primaries is June 12, although same-day registration is also available with ID.
  - Mail-in ballots will be mailed on June 2 if you are already registered
- Kevin O'Leary's gargantuan data center north of the Great Salt Lake which is a rare bipartisan issue was approved by commissioners on May 4 with no public comment.
  - The only path left to block it is a county referendum which requires 5,400+ signatures. If you live in Box Elder and care about this issue or the process it was handled, this is a critical time to get your signature counted.
- Resigned and disgraced US congressman, Jason Cheffetz, and other republicans are lining up to run for the Governorship in 2028 because Governor Cox has announced he does not intend to run again.
