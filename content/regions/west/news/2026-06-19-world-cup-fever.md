# **Colorado**

## **📰  News**

- ​1st Congressional District Venue Cancellations
	- The Democratic primary between 30-year incumbent Rep. Diana DeGette and first-time challenger Melat Kiros drew eyes this last weekend - A planned Kiros campaign rally featuring leftist streamer Hasan Piker faced consecutive [cancellations](https://denverite.com/2026/06/15/melat-kiros-hasan-piker-ogden-theatre-canceled/) from three Denver venues, ReelWorks, Stanley Marketplace, and the Ogden Theatre.
	- The Ogden cited "pressure and concern from the local community" less than 48 hours before the event. Kiros publicly accused DeGette of utilizing her "donor class to silence us." DeGette's camp pushed back, criticizing Kiros for bringing in a controversial figure like Piker. 
	- Notably, the Ogden Theatre is operated by [AEG Presents](https://coloradotimesrecorder.com/2026/06/davis-anschutz-linked-venues-tried-and-failed-to-block-a-progressive-political-rally/79727/), owned by billionaire Phil Anschutz—a prominent political donor whom the Kiros campaign accused of deliberately meddling to suppress their event. The rally was ultimately relocated to the State Capitol steps, though Piker backed out of attending in person due to safety concerns. 
- ​Attorney General Primary Debate
	- A recent [forum](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c8Ol_Rld3z8) was held for the Democratic candidates vying for the open Colorado Attorney General seat Currently held by gubernatorial candidate Phil Wiser.
	- A notable absence from the debate stage was Secretary of State Jena Griswold, who has faced ongoing scrutiny regarding a late-2024 security [incident](https://coloradonewsline.com/briefs/jena-griswold-questioned-by-colorado-lawmakers-over-voting-systems-password-leak/) where her office accidentally left a spreadsheet containing voting machine passwords on a public website for several months. While her office maintained the error did not directly compromise election systems due to other security layers, the controversy remains a recurring talking point in statewide races.  
- U.S. Senate Primary Challenge
	- Incumbent U.S. Senator John Hickenlooper has been [notably absent](https://coloradonewsline.com/2026/06/17/colorado-senate-primary-hickenlooper-declines-to-debate/) from recent campaign activities. This is despite, or perhaps in light of still retaining a nearly [$3 Million](https://www.coloradopolitics.com/2026/06/17/hickenlooper-raises-900k-since-april-heads-into-primary-with-almost-3-million-on-hand/) war chest. 
	- He is currently facing a serious primary challenge from State Senator Julie Gonzales, who represents Denver's District 34.
	- The two took distinctly different paths to the June 30 primary ballot: Hickenlooper collected petition signatures to guarantee his spot and bypassed the party assembly process. Conversely, Gonzales secured her ballot access by [overwhelmingly winning](https://kiowacountypress.net/content/weiser-gonzales-take-top-spots-colorados-2026-democratic-primary-ballot) the Colorado Democratic Party's statewide assembly with over 74% of the delegate vote.
