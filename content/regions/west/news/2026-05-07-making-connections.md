# 🌊 West Region Dispatch – Making Connections

Going to start this week's dispatch with amplifying calls from the leadership team to remind ourselves that we are an organization that is focused on pragmatism, and building broad coalitions, which separates us from other orgs who get mired in purity testing. As the organization continues to grow, we are starting to see some occasional heated ideological arguments in the various channels and voice chats. Since many of us come from Destiny's community which leans more to the center than much of the online left, we're bound to have some disagreements from the more progressive and further left leaning members that are joining our ranks.

But while disagreements are fine, open hostility is not, because we are trying to build a big tent. That means working alongside people we may not agree with on every issue, strategy, or worldview. Building a broad coalition is difficult, messy, and often frustrating, but it's also necessary if we want to build durable political power. We have to learn how to effectively organize under one tent while staying focused on the larger mission against the illiberal forces currently encroaching upon all our lives.

## 🌲 Washington

### 📋 PCO Filing Deadline

- Washington state candidate filing closes tomorrow at 8PM.

If you live in Washington, we highly encourage you to consider running for Precinct Committee Officer (PCO)

- PCOs help get out the vote
- Talk to neighbors and local voters
- Participate in county and legislative district meetings
- Help recommend appointments when legislative vacancies occur

Additional details:

- Terms last two years
- No filing fee required
- Many precincts currently have only one filer or no PCO at all

The Washington State Democratic Party site provides a handbook with additional information for anyone interested.
