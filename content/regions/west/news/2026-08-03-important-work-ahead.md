## **🇺🇸 Finding Common Cause**

Last edition I postponed writing about my Libcon experience, because I was asked to delay mentioning certain items publicly until this week. Unfortunately I'm still being asked to put that on hold so it won't be published this edition either. Sorry for the tease to anyone who might have been interested, but if you're curious you can always just ask me personally! 

So instead of a full travel log, in light of all the recent left-liberal infighting, I'm going to relay what Bill Kristol said in his closing remarks at Libcon. He said that when he was starting off at *The Bulwark*, he received a surprising e-mail from James Carville. In the e-mail, Carville talked about his journey from being a poor kid growing up in Baton Rouge, Louisiana to reaching the heights of US politics, most notably as Bill Clinton's lead strategist in his 1992 presidential campaign.

But he said that it all pales in comparison to what he's fighting for now. For him, joining with former political and ideological rivals he used to clash with (in this case Bill Kristol) in order to fight back against the country's slide toward illiberal authoritarianism is more meaningful than all that he's done before. It's literally that "never thought I'd be fighting alongside" LOTR meme in real life.

However you may feel about Bill Kristol or James Carville, at the very least hopefully you can feel inspiration in uniting together, despite many differences, under a common cause that is more important than all the petty squabbles that came before.
