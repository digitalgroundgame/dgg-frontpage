This year, our collective efforts yielded extraordinary results across multiple fronts: we successfully phonebanked, interviewed candidates throughout the entire region, wrote and mailed postcards, and personally knocked on 5,975 doors. That level of action is truly amazing. We actively supported candidates across the entire political spectrum ranging from safe districts to highly competitive flippable seats, and even deeply red strongholds. Every single one of those districts saw meaningful movement. Some key seats were successfully held or newly converted to blue, while in others, our dedicated outreach moved the needle, laying crucial groundwork for future victories. (Red is, in fact, my favorite shade of blue.) 

For the remainder of the year, our primary focus will shift toward strengthening our local community infrastructure, as a deep-rooted grassroots organization this is how we drive sustainable, real change. Anyone who attended our joint event with SwingLeft in Bristol, PA, to participate in deep canvassing witnessed firsthand the remarkable power of authentic, one-on-one voter conversations, an approach that directly contributed to Bob Harvie flipping PA-01. Ensuring that community members are registered and prepared to vote remains a vital, year-round commitment, regardless of whether it is a major national cycle or a minor election year. As we look ahead to next year, we are preparing for numerous local races that are every bit as critical to our community's future. Thank you all for your time, your commitment and your energy to make remarkable changes. We have three trifectas left to fight for in the region (looking at you Vermont, New Hampshire and West Virginia) and better day to bring to these United States.
