## Maine

Maine governor Janet Mills suspended her campaign on Thursday, saying she no longer had the financial resources to compete against Graham Platner. "While I have the drive and passion, commitment and experience, and above all else -- the fight -- to continue on, I very simply do not have the one thing that political campaigns unfortunately require today: the financial resources." While She did not mention or endorse Mr.

Platner in a recent interview on Monday, a spokesman for Mills said that she has never voted for and will not vote for Susan Collins in this election either adding that she will continue to watch how Platner will earn the support of Maine's voters.

Article from the [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/)

## New York

New York State Democrats are once again pushing to reform the states Congressional districts. This conversation is rising again as an urgent issue to tackle in the state legislator after Supreme Court ruling and many states rush to partisan gerrymandering. This was a topic of discussion last summer in Albany, but in light of these recent events Democrats are pushing to make the required state constitutional amendment quickly.

It is already too late for the 2026 midterm map to be impacted, and there are only a few weeks to make a change in time to redraw for the 2028 election.

Article from [Spectrum News](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/)

## Washington D.C.

Ballots for the Democratic primary go out in two weeks, with the final date for submission a month from now. In addition to the heavily contested mayoral race, the Ward 1 City Council Race has one of the largest fields of candidates of any race. There are five candidates vying for the seat with over half of voters undecided.
