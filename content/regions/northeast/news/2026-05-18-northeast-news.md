## Vermont

The Vermont House of Representatives has approved a proposed constitutional amendment to add an "equal protection under the law" clause to the state constitution, extending protections to nine classes of people, including race, sex, disability, gender identity, and sexual orientation. The measure is slated to appear on the November general election ballot. Vermont stands out as one of the few states without an explicit equal protection clause in its constitution, making this proposal a significant shift in how rights are safeguarded at the state level.

Supporters say the amendment is not about expanding rights but about strengthening and clarifying them, creating a more "durable civil rights policy." By enshrining equal protection in the constitution, advocates argue, it would provide clearer, lasting protections against discrimination across state action and government policy for the nine protected groups.

Article from [Vermont Republic](https://www.vermontpublic.org/local-news/2026-05-13/proposed-constitutional-amendment-voters-november)

## New York

LIRR shuts down due to union strike for the first time in over 30 years. After unions and MTA failed to come to an agreement by 12:01 AM on 5/16/26, the unions that make up the majority of the LIRR workforce triggering the strike. The quarter million daily commuters of countries busiest commuter rail system will be without train service until an agreement is made. As of 9 AM on 5/16/26 no new talks between the MTA and the unions have been scheduled. Shuttle busses will run with limited service to mitigate the damage caused by the disruption. The strike is on a dispute in pay rates for the next 4 years contract, but the MTA Chair they will not put an undo burden on tax payers for what are already the highest paid rail workers in the nation.

Article from [Gothamist](https://gothamist.com/news/lirr-workers-go-on-strike-shut-down-nations-busiest-commuter-train-line)
