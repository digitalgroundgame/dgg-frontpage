Over the past decade, Ohio’s fracking industry has exploded along its eastern border. Washington County alone has more than 15,000 active operations. A proposal was submitted by the BLM in 2020 to lease 40,000 acres of Wayne National Forest (Ohio’s only National Forest). This was struck down by a federal judge that year, but a near identical proposal was recommended by the Biden admin in 2024.Ohio residents have complained about the very loose regulations surrounding fracking operations, with some residents having upwards of 100 active operations within a 5 mile radius of their home. The volume of fracking has not only threatened the aquifers in the area, but has caused significant traffic, noise, and ecological issues. The non-profit Save Ohio Parks has been fighting against the exploitation of the state’s natural resources since the explosion of drilling starting in 2010. Here are the options you can act on today!

1. Save Ohio Parks has created the brochure below to call for a moratorium on these fracking leases, [click here to print and use it](https://saveohioparks.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/moratorium-flyer-print-pdf.pdf)!
2. Spread the word on fracking by printing and spreading out an infographic created for general education on fracking and its effects on Ohioans! [Click here to get a copy!](https://saveohioparks.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/NewSaveOhioParksBrochureMay2025.pdf)
3. Sign up for the Save Ohio Parks' email list to be sent their own CTA's regarding fracking on public lands! [Click here to sign up!](https://saveohioparks.org/join-us/)
