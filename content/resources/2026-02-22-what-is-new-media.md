This section is designed for people who are largely unfamiliar with the space. If you know what a skibidi toilet is, you can skip it.

In a political context, "Traditional" and "New" media refer loosely to the different ways people have kept a pulse on the "facts of the matter" of what is happening in the world, and the narratives and perspectives that contextualize them. Traditional media is composed of a variety of distinct types of content: newspaper articles, 24 hour news channels, documentary films, or even shows like "West Wing".

There are a few distinct types of content that define the New Media landscape:

## Podcasts

Podcasts are generally 1-3 hour conversations in person with one or multiple hosts. They are functionally similar to interviews, but often have a more laid back, authentic, conversational vibe. High viewership and watch-hours coupled with a relatively low production cost has made them some of the most engaging content in the space.

- [Joe Rogan Experience](https://www.youtube.com/@joerogan/videos) is the king of podcasts, often breaking 1M views per episode.
- [Gavin Newsom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yGooLJoyrI4) has been leading the charge into new media with his own podcast.

## Mid-length or "YouTube" Videos

This is a bucket comprising a tremendous variety of shows with different lengths, styles, content types, and view counts. Mid-length videos are primarily hosted on YouTube.

- [Destiny](https://www.youtube.com/@destiny) is a variety creator that uploads [debates](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=51gcd9uUwGY&t=9869s), [interviews](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y2BbtmvjkdQ&t=50s), commentary on current events, video game content, and more.
- [Brian Tyler Cohen](https://www.youtube.com/@briantylercohen/videos) operates similarly to a news station, hosting a large number of daily interviews, discussions, and segments on notable topics.

## Live Streams

Live streamers broadcast live to thousands of people. A great YouTube video can get hundreds of thousands of views, while a great livestream has tens of thousands of live viewers. While there are fewer live viewers, they are much more invested. The stream often serves as a primary social community for these highly invested users, where they chat in real time with other viewers.

- Destiny and Atrioc both run livestreams, then cut key segments into videos.

## Shorts

Short content is generally under 1 minute long, giving just enough time to make one quick joke or argument. They are generally consumed in infinitely scrolling platforms like Tiktok. These videos get significantly more views, but generally have lower impact and engagement.

- [Dean Withers](https://www.tiktok.com/@itsdeaann?lang=en) frequently gets millions of views on his shorts.
