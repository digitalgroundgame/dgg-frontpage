# Inflection Point

Consider a world where everyone who believes in the project of Liberalism, in our constitution, in enlightenment values cancelled all of their media appearances and stopped giving interviews. A world where each time slot was filled, instead, with conspiracy theorists, [sex traffickers](https://x.com/i/status/1877283622127821308) and [Putinist stooges](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DDc4dadmjNs). It's a hard world for some to imagine, but it is the world that young Americans grow up in. At 29, this is the only media environment I have known.

Youtube, the primary place where my generation consumes long-form video, is dominated by figures like Tim Pool, who took [millions of dollars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Tenet_Media_investigation) from Russia before declaring that "[Ukraine is the enemy of this country](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DDc4dadmjNs)". Or Russell Brand, a likely [serial sex offender](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/comedian-russell-brand-charged-with-multiple-counts-of-rape-and-sexual-assault) who, fortunately, finally [found Jesus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XVs1mslJbg4) on stage with pundit Tucker Carlson.

On October 17th, [four of the five largest podcasts](https://www.reddit.com/r/YAPms/comments/1o9j0rd/3_of_the_5_largest_podcasts_4_if_you_count_rogan/) were political, anti-establishment, conspiracy theorists, and anti-institutional voices. Each of them has millions of listeners. For every voice willing to put in a good word for the most prosperous society in human history and the systems that carved it, there are five who baselessly denigrate it, getting triple the attention. But these positive voices do exist. [Destiny](https://www.youtube.com/@destiny), [Dean Withers](https://www.tiktok.com/@itsdeaann?lang=en), [Atrioc](https://www.youtube.com/@AtriocClips/videos), [Adam Mockler](https://www.youtube.com/@adammockler), [Pondering Politics](https://www.youtube.com/@ponderingpolitics/videos), [BTC](https://www.youtube.com/briantylercohen), and dozens of others each draw hundreds of thousands of viewers every day, and are growing.

If the goal of this paper was to draw attention to a coming inflection point and prepare for it, I would have noted that their combined viewership rivals major news stations, or contrasted their exponential growth against legacy media's decline. If the goal of this paper was to warn of a change that was coming, I would have made these arguments in 2016. But change is here. "New media" has subsumed legacy media completely.

The time for warnings came and went. This paper instead serves as a post mortem. Any 3 of the top 30 podcasts together rival the viewership of any [declining news network](https://cordcuttersnews.com/fox-news-msnbc-cnn-all-saw-their-ratings-drop-in-the-3rd-quarter-of-2025-by-as-much-as-42/). Taken as a whole, new media now totally eclipses legacy media in viewership and cultural impact. And during this rise to prominence, the primary space where people under 35 access information has been abandoned by people who share our values. MAGA leadership calls into these online shows every single day, while trustworthy experts are nowhere to be found.

Young people, especially young men, feel abandoned by liberalism because they have been.

They feel forgotten because they are forgotten.

They abandon us because we abandoned them.

It's time to set things right.

## How did we get here?

When Joe Rogan started what would become the largest podcast on earth, he was LA-based, pro immigrant amnesty, pro drug legalization, but largely apolitical and non-partisan. Over the past 10 years, he has flipped many of these positions and moved to Texas, after interviewing dozens of Republicans, including most of Trump's current cabinet, JD Vance, and Trump himself. The few Democrat-aligned officials who have come on this show, like [Bernie Sanders](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mYVzme2fybU) or [John Fetterman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_y-59phRHRM), are generally seen as fringe parts of the party, not its core. His shift from independence to the right culminated with an endorsement of Trump in 2024.

This pattern repeats across the entire space. Right wing creators are universally supported and bolstered: Tim Pool [interviewed Lara Trump](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dcCbyCLTg9U), and he accepted [2 million dollars from Russia](https://www.cnn.com/2024/09/13/media/right-wing-media-influencers-tenet-russian-money) last year.

Right wing media is bolstered by institutional support.

Independent media shifts right as right wing perspectives flood their guestlists.

And left-of-center aligned media is stifled by a lack of access to institutional support.

## How do we fix this?

MAGA is able to message to everyone, everywhere, on every issue, every day. Democrats seem to be stuck waiting for someone, somewhere to craft silver-bullet healthcare messaging, which would need to be somehow appealing to both healthy middle class 19 year olds and people who rely on it to survive, and to share this messaging exclusively through extremely friendly, but lower-viewership platforms.

We don't need to worry about the party line or the national brand. We need officials to speak authentically and openly and often about whatever they care about, whatever is important to them. Almost as important as the position is just seeing a human face that believes in something. Algorithms in new media will automatically put your messaging in front of audiences that resonate with them.

It is a good thing that we do not tolerate lunatics on our side. We don't want to completely throw our standards out the window as MAGA has. But we need to acknowledge that we have been far, far too skittish about working with people who have personal blemishes.

## Authenticity & Expectations

The faltering political career of George Romney, father of Mitt Romney, is famously attributed to his ["Brainwashing" gaffe](https://www.minnpost.com/eric-black-ink/2011/12/politics-gaffe-recalling-brainwashing-george-romney/) in 1968. An environment in which a single slip up could end a promising career naturally created a culture focused around caution and risk aversion. However, the opposite seems true today: my MAGA dad went from thinking Hunter Biden was a corrupt villain to seeing him as an intelligent and virtuous guy after media appearances where, among other things, Biden mentioned that he "probably smoked more Parmesan cheese than anyone that you know" while picking through carpets for "anything that looked like crack cocaine."

Authenticity is the lifeblood of the New Media. When I knocked 10,000 doors for Harris in 2024, a vast number of Trump's supporters echoed the sentiment that they "knew what kind of asshole he was" and that "at least he was honest about it."

In order to thrive in the New Media environment, creators give honest, off-the-cuff takes every day, for hours a day, for years. The kind of takes that may have ended careers in 1968 are a daily occurrence on these platforms, and the worst moments of thousands of hours of filming, many of which have been misconstrued or recanted, will be perpetually weaponized by our political enemies to ensure the space remains radioactive to officials operating under 1968's norms.

The truth is that with a long enough career, everyone is going to have some controversy.
